About TOTAL RESOURCES

Built Around Industry.

Designed for Long-Term Value.

以产业为根基 · 为长期价值而构建

TOTAL RESOURCES HOLDING LTD. (CHONGQING) is an industrial resources integration and value creation platform headquartered in Chongqing, China.

We connect industrial resources, markets, capital, technology and operating capabilities to develop sustainable businesses, supply chains and industrial projects.

Our focus is not simply on identifying opportunities, but on turning them into executable projects, operating businesses and long-term value.

Company Profile

Connecting Resources. Creating Value.

连接资源 · 创造价值

Industry, capital, markets and operations connected through one platform.

TOTAL RESOURCES operates at the intersection of industry, investment, supply chains, project development and technology.

Our role is to identify valuable industrial opportunities, organize the resources required to execute them, and build the commercial and operating structures necessary for long-term development.

Rather than approaching investment, trade and project development as separate activities, we integrate them into a coordinated industrial platform.

Resources create potential. Markets create direction. Operations create value.

Operating Philosophy

Operations Before Expansion. Markets Before Capacity.

运营先于扩张 · 市场先于产能

We believe industrial development should begin with real market demand and operating capability, rather than capacity expansion alone.

Our approach emphasizes market validation, operating structures, supply-chain coordination and commercial sustainability before large-scale capital deployment.

This operating-first approach helps align investment, supply chains and project development with sustainable commercial demand.

Capital serves industry. Technology supports operations. Resources create value only when they are connected to sustainable markets.
