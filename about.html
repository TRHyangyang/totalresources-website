Company Profile

Connecting Resources. Creating Value.

连接资源 · 创造价值

TOTAL RESOURCES HOLDING LTD. (CHONGQING)

TOTAL RESOURCES is an industrial resources integration and value creation platform based in Chongqing, China.

The company focuses on energy and industrial supply chains, industrial investment, project development, modern agriculture and strategic resource integration.

Through market-oriented operations and long-term partnerships, TOTAL RESOURCES connects resources, capital, technology, supply chains and project execution capabilities.

重庆首胜能源科技有限公司定位于产业资源整合与价值创造平台， 重点布局能源与工业品供应链、产业投资、项目开发、 现代农业及产业资源整合。
