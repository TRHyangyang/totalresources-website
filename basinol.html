A German manufacturing heritage

Engineering experience built over generations.

Finke Mineralölwerk GmbH was founded in Germany in 1884 and has developed, produced and marketed high-quality lubricants through successive generations of industrial technology.

BASINOL® builds on this German lubricant manufacturing heritage, combining formulation expertise, quality management and practical industrial experience.

BASINOL® 巴斯龙依托德国长期积累的润滑制造传统， 将配方技术、质量管理与工业应用经验结合， 为现代汽车与工业设备提供专业润滑解决方案。
