Brand Story

品牌故事

BASINOL® originates from Germany's long-standing lubricant manufacturing tradition, focusing on technological innovation, product reliability and application performance.

BASINOL® 巴斯龙源自德国长期积累的润滑油制造传统， 专注于技术创新、产品可靠性和工业应用性能。

The brand combines advanced formulation technology with practical industrial experience to meet the requirements of modern machinery and vehicles.

品牌结合先进配方技术与工业应用经验， 满足现代机械设备和车辆的高性能需求。
