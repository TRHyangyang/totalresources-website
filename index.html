About Us 关于我们

TOTAL RESOURCES HOLDING LTD. (CHONGQING) is a global industrial resource integration and supply chain operation platform.

重庆首胜能源科技有限公司是一家面向全球的产业资源整合与供应链运营平台。

Based in Chongqing, China, the company focuses on energy resources, industrial products, supply chain solutions and sustainable industrial development.

公司立足重庆，围绕能源产业、 工业产品供应链、 可持续资源开发及产业创新解决方案开展业务。
