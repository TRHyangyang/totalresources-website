TOTAL RESOURCES HOLDING LTD. (CHONGQING) is a global industrial resource integration and supply chain operation platform.

重庆首胜能源科技有限公司是一家面向全球的产业资源整合与供应链运营平台。

Based in Chongqing, China, the company focuses on energy resources, industrial products, global supply chains and sustainable development opportunities.

公司立足重庆，围绕能源资源、工业产品、 全球供应链以及可持续产业发展开展业务。
