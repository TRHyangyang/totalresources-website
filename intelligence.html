ENERGY INTELLIGENCE

Oil Daily & Energy Markets.

原油与能源产业链研究

Energy Intelligence tracks crude oil fundamentals, inventories, OPEC+, positioning, physical flows, refining and petrochemical transmission.

The objective is not merely to explain price movements, but to identify changes in market structure and industrial impact.
